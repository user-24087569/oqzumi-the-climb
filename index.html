Privacy Policy

App: OQZUMI - Brain Training (package: com.veenslay.oqzumi)
Last updated: 24 September 2026

In short: OQZUMI does not collect, share or sell your personal information. The game works offline, has no ads, no analytics and no account. Everything it remembers stays on your device.

1. Who we are

OQZUMI - Brain Training ("the app") is a brain-training game published by Veenslay ("we", "us"). Contact: business.naveenpandey@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any account details, and the app does not use the microphone or camera.

3. Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the app saves the following only on your device, using the phone's local storage:

  • the name you give your in-game companion;
  • your progress, best level, practice levels and game statistics;
  • your in-game coins, purchased themes and personal best times;
  • your settings (sound, music, vibration, theme).

This data is never sent to us or to any third party. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

4. Ads, analytics and third parties

The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools, and it does not connect to any server of ours. Zumi Coins are earned only by playing; they cannot be bought with real money, and the app has no in-app purchases.

5. Permissions

The app uses vibration (haptic feedback) for game feel, and plays sound. It does not request access to your location, contacts, storage, camera or microphone.

6. Children

The app is suitable for general audiences. Because we collect no personal data from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data sharing and security

We do not share, sell or transfer any data, because we do not receive any. Your saved progress lives in your device's private app storage, protected by the phone's operating system.

8. Your choices

You can reset all saved data at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings > Apps > OQZUMI > Storage, or by uninstalling the app.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy (for example, if we add ads or online features), we will update this page and the date above before releasing that version.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: business.naveenpandey@gmail.com
